## Safety and academic evidence still required

For the confirmed school pair/pathway, seek recent multiyear academic progress and student safety/belonging survey results, with response counts and suppression limits. Separate elementary, middle-age and secondary evidence. Do not infer low drug/gang risk from the absence of news articles or from neighbourhood affluence.

For an eventual school visit/contact: ask how repeat bullying is tracked, how quickly parents are notified, how retaliation is monitored, who supervises lunch/playgrounds, counselling availability, transition support, and how drug/gang recruitment concerns are addressed at later ages. Record responses as reported, with date; do not contact schools without authorization.

The six-year-old may have several years before middle-school transition. Catchments, capacity, staff and school openings can change; current assignment is not a future guarantee.
