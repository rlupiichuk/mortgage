# Household criteria

Updated: 2026-10-25

## Confirmed by the user

| Item | Current understanding |
|---|---|
| Purpose | Explore possible purchase plans; revisit the decision and research periodically |
| Region | Greater Vancouver, explicitly including Langley City and Township |
| Household income | Approximately $180,000/year before tax |
| Debt | None reported |
| Down payment | Approximately $250,000, separate from emergency and closing-cost savings |
| Monthly target | Mortgage below approximately $3,500; a larger payment is acceptable if rent covers part |
| Child | Age six at the time of discussion |
| First priority | School safety: bullying, drugs, gang activity, and surrounding environment |
| Second priority | Academics, through elementary and secondary-school ages |
| Outdoor space | Backyard preferred; size and privacy requirements not yet specified |
| Nearby parks | Nice-to-have preference; target parks accessible within a 20–30 minute walk; track child-friendly features and safe crossings |

| Commute | Ideally downtown Vancouver in about an hour by public transit; flexible |

## Not yet confirmed

- Whether a townhouse or duplex with a private yard is acceptable versus detached only.
- School comparison now focuses on elementary and secondary, per clarification; a separate middle-school column is not required.
- Purchase timing, expected holding period, first-time buyer status, residency/status relevant to financing and taxes.
- Income composition and stability (employment/self-employment, bonuses, number of earners).
- Desired bedrooms, usable interior area, yard size, parking, and renovation tolerance.
- Preferred park features (playground, trails, sports fields); walking target is now 20–30 minutes.
- Acceptable residual traffic noise when a home is buffered from a busy road; one street away is acceptable, with no fixed distance cutoff specified.
- Emergency reserve size and ability to carry a larger mortgage without rent.
- Whether the $3,500 after-rent target should be measured after tax and all rental expenses.
- Academic priorities and any learning, language, or support needs.

## Research interpretation, not a user commitment

Compare a home without rental income against a home with an existing permitted suite. Use conservative rental cash flow and evaluate school fit before making location recommendations.

See also the [viewing checklist](viewing-checklist.md) for what to verify before, during and after a viewing.
