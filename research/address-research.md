# Address research follow-up

Researched 2026-10-25. This pass investigates every previously blank park, street-setting and commute block. Remaining unknowns are explicit verification tasks, not cleared criteria.

## Method and confidence

BC address geocoder coordinates; OSRM/OpenStreetMap network routes where returned successfully. Router times are model estimates, not observed trips, live traffic, accessibility audits or confirmed public access. Parcel points and block/intersection destinations can snap to another street or side of a park. Walking ranges use distance at 3–4.5 km/h, with 2–5 minutes access allowance. When routing failed, a separate rough range uses straight-line distance times 1.3–1.8, not a measured route.

Commute ranges are approximate weekday morning planning estimates to Waterfront Station. They are not live traffic predictions, observed journeys or exact trip-planner outputs. Driving ranges exclude parking; transit ranges include broad access/wait/transfer allowances. Return/off-peak journeys may differ. No future SkyTrain extension is counted.

[Official WCE timetable](https://www.translink.ca/translink/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express): Port Coquitlam 36 minutes, Pitt Meadows 46 minutes and Port Haney 56 minutes to Waterfront, station to station. Directional weekday peak service; access is additional. [555 route](https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/route/555/direction/0/schedule?page=1), [351 route](https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/route/351/direction/0/map), [503 route map](https://infomaps.translink.ca/Route_Diagrams/210/r503.pdf). These support route options, not exact full-journey durations.

Six walking routes and five driving baselines returned valid results. Other requests failed or returned unusable responses; no invented router result is substituted.

## What changed the screen

- P04 (Walnut Grove): arterial-site flag on 208 Street. Unit 9 may be buffered inside the complex; a site plan is needed. [Township corridor plan](https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Neighbourhood-Plan-Bylaws/Carvolth-Neighbourhood-Plan-%28No.-4995%29.pdf).
- P12 (Langley City): 208 Street arterial frontage is a material quiet-street concern. [City transportation plan](https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/langley-city-transportation-2050-final-draftpdf).
- P10 (Aldergrove): Philip Jackman Park estimate straddles the 30-minute target, so access is conditional. A nearer school playfield is not assumed to be an unrestricted public park.
- P02: [SD43 capital-project schedule](https://www.sd43.bc.ca/capital-projects) says Fall 2027 for Burke Mountain Secondary, replacing older 2026 marketing. Address assignment/transition remains unconfirmed.
- P06: [2006 Surrey report](https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/RPT_2006_R025.pdf) recommended approving a strata conversion at the exact duplex address. It is historical application evidence, not proof of current registered title, final approval or present shared obligations. Do not infer that “no strata fees” means no shared repair duties.

Exact address listing sources are recorded in property-sample.json; all sample homes now have an advertised annual tax amount, with historical years flagged. Its planning figure uses a $0 mean from three King George Corridor half-duplex advertisements claiming no fees, with sources in [cost-estimates.json](cost-estimates.json). This is a small selected advertised sample across mixed ages/sizes, not a representative neighbourhood average or verified strata budget. The original null remains intact. A maintenance reserve is added separately.

## Remaining verification

- Exact townhouse front door/yard position, rear and side exposure; park entrances, gates, sidewalk continuity and safe crossings. Geocodes can be parcel/block points; route snapping may start from another side of a complex.
- School assignment/capacity via district address lookup; several locator pages require interactive CAPTCHA. Policy documents cannot establish comparable incident frequency or response effectiveness. No automatic school safety clearance.
- Timed transit itineraries for a fixed workday arrival, return and off-peak travel; road congestion at realistic departure times.
- Current strata documents, insurance, tax notices, suite permits and existing leases. Unknown cost estimates remain labelled and replaceable.
