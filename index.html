Schools: next evidence checks

  • Confirm the elementary and later-school pathway using the official district locator.
  • Compare multiyear learning progress and student safety/belonging results where available, including sample sizes.
  • Understand bullying escalation, supervision, counselling and transition support.
  • Check capacity and future catchment changes.

Walnut Grove examples include nearby K–7 elementaries; a separate middle school cannot be assumed. Absence of news is not proof of low drug or gang risk.
